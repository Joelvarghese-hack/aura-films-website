Gananoque, Ontario

Photography in Gananoque.

Gananoque is 25 minutes from our studio in Kingston: the Thousand Islands, the river and a main street that photographs better than it has any right to.

Photographing in Gananoque

Gananoque weddings often run to island venues, which means timings are tied to boats. Tell us that early and we will build the day around it.

Prices and travel

Prices in Gananoque are the same as in Kingston: portraits from $85, family sessions from $179, events from $399 and weddings from $1,195. Travel is included, because Gananoque is within an hour of the studio.

Albin photographs every session himself and edits every frame by hand, with galleries delivered in ten to twenty-one days.

Questions about Gananoque

Do you charge travel to Gananoque?
No. Gananoque is 25 minutes from Kingston, and travel within an hour is included in every package.
Where are the best places for photos in Gananoque?
The waterfront and the marina at the end of the day, Confederation Park, and the stretch of King Street where the light bounces off the shopfronts. The islands themselves if you have a boat organised.
Can you photograph an island wedding in the Thousand Islands?
Yes. Island venues run on boat times, so tell us early and we will build the photography timeline around the crossings.
Who will take our photographs?
Albin, the photographer behind Aura Films, at every session. Larger weddings and the Elite event package add a second photographer.
What if the weather is bad?
Outdoor portrait and family sessions move to another day at no cost. Weddings go ahead, with covered and indoor options found in advance.
