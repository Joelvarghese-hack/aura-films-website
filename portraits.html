Before your session

How much is a portrait session in Kingston?
$85 for thirty minutes, $165 for an hour, or $250 for up to two hours with several looks or locations. Extra time is $15 per half hour.
What should I wear for my photoshoot?
Clothes you already feel good in. Solid colours and textures photograph better than small patterns. Send photos of your options beforehand and we will help you choose.
Which session is right for a headshot?
The thirty-minute Quick Shoot: one look and fifteen edited photographs.
What happens if it rains?
We move outdoor sessions to another day at no cost.
When do I get my photos?
Ten to twenty-one days, in an online gallery with a print release.
