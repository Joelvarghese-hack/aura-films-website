Accessibility

Last updated · September 2026

Aura Films wants everyone to be able to browse our work, read our prices and get in touch, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

1. Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We test the site with automated accessibility tools and by keyboard, and we fix what we find.

2. What the site does

  • Text alternatives that describe each photograph.
  • Full keyboard access, a skip link, visible focus and labelled form fields.
  • Text and background colours that meet WCAG contrast ratios.
  • Animation that switches off when your device’s “reduce motion” setting is on.
  • Layouts that adapt from small phones to large screens.

3. Known limitations

Our optional booking calendar is provided by Calendly, and we do not control its accessibility. If it does not work for you, email or call us and we will book you directly.

4. Feedback and other formats

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you would like information in another format, contact us at itsaurafilms@gmail.com or 343 989 4546. We reply within 24 to 48 hours.
