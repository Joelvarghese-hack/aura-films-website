Which package fits

Two hours suits a shower or birthday that happens in one room. Four hours suits a party with speeches, cake and a long evening. Six hours with two photographers is for large, busy events where the guests are as much the story as the hosts.

Planning your event

How much does an event photographer cost in Kingston?
$399 for up to two hours, $665 for up to four, or $935 for up to six hours with two photographers. Extra time is $120 to $175 an hour depending on the package.
How many hours do I need for a baby shower?
Usually two. Showers tend to happen in one room over a short afternoon, and the Essentials package covers it.
How fast can we get the photos?
Fourteen to twenty-one days on Essentials, ten to fourteen on Signature with a sneak peek, and within seven days on Elite.
Do you photograph corporate events?
Yes. Tell us how the photographs will be used and we will make sure the gallery covers it.
What do you need from us beforehand?
The timeline and the names of the people who matter most, so they are photographed.
