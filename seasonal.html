Questions about the minis

How much is a mini session?
$85 for the fall dates and $100 for the holiday and snow dates. Either way you get twenty minutes, twelve photographs edited by hand and up to four people. If you love more of the gallery, ten more edited photographs are $75.
How do I book a slot?
Pick a date and a time in the form below. We confirm by email within a day, with the payment details. Your slot is held once it is paid, first come, first served. Each day has six slots.
Where are the sessions?
In Kingston. Albin picks one spot for each day, the week before, based on where the light and the season look best. You get the exact location and parking with your confirmation.
Will there be snow?
For the January dates, almost certainly. In all sixteen winters with records since 2009, Kingston had snow on the ground every day of that week. For the December dates, it is closer to a coin toss, which is why those are holiday sessions first and snow sessions only if the weather obliges.
What if the weather is bad?
If it rains, or Environment Canada issues a cold or freezing-rain warning for Kingston, we move your session to the weather date at the same time, free. Ordinary cold is not a reason to move: dress for it and we keep the session moving.
Can I bring more than four people, or a dog?
Minis are built for up to four people. For a bigger group, book a regular family session. Ask about a dog when you book and we will tell you whether the location suits one.
What if I cannot make it?
You can hand your slot to a friend. Slots are not refundable, because each one is a set time on a set day that we cannot resell at short notice.
When do we get the photos?
Within ten days, in an online gallery. Sessions on December 12 and 13 come back to you before Christmas.
