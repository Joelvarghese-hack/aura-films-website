2Balance. The balance is due on or before the session date unless we agree otherwise in writing. Final images are released once the balance is paid in full. All prices are in Canadian dollars. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

Initials
5Rescheduling. You may reschedule once with reasonable notice, subject to availability, and your retainer carries over. Further changes may carry a small administration fee. Outdoor sessions moved because of unsafe weather are always rescheduled free of charge.

Refunds are not given for differences in personal taste; if a delivered image has a genuine technical fault we will re-edit it or, where reasonably possible, reshoot.
