Frequently Asked
Questions

→ How long have you been a photographer?›

I've been photographing weddings and portraits professionally for over 7 years, based in Kingston but available across Ontario and beyond.

→ Will I receive all the images unedited?›

No — you'll receive a fully edited, curated gallery of all the best images. Raw, unedited files are not included, as careful editing is part of the final product and my artistic vision.

→ Do you offer print packages?›

Yes! Fine art albums, prints, and wall art collections are available as add-ons. I work with premium print labs to ensure heirloom-quality results that last a lifetime.

→ How long does it take to receive the photos?›

Typically 4–8 weeks for wedding galleries, and 2–3 weeks for portrait and couples sessions. I always aim to deliver sooner when possible.

→ What is your photography style?›

My style is candid, documentary, and editorial — warm, timeless, and full of real emotion. I lean into natural light and honest moments rather than heavily posed or over-directed imagery.

→ What is your payment policy?›

A 25% retainer is due upon booking to secure your date, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your event. I accept e-transfer and major credit cards.

→ What if I need to cancel or reschedule due to unforeseen causes?›

I understand that life happens. Rescheduling is possible depending on availability at no additional charge. Cancellation policies are outlined in the contract. I always aim to be as flexible and understanding as possible.
