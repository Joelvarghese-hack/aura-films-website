About Us

Two Lenses,
One Aura

We are a Toronto-based film production studio driven by an obsession with craft. Every project begins with a single question: what is the soul of this story?

From intimate wedding films to ambitious brand campaigns and documentary work, we bring the same cinematic rigour to every frame. We don't believe in ordinary.

Aura Films is built on a deep respect for the image — the way light falls, the way a moment holds its breath, the way a cut can shift the weight of a scene.
